Kentro Capital · Internal comms

Intranet redesign & “Shout Out”

InsightKentro’s three businesses worked in silos, and low page views showed how disconnected colleagues felt.
ApproachRebuilt the intranet in the newly approved brand, and worked with every team to refresh their pages. I added “Shout Out” peer praise to every team page and the homepage, so colleagues could see and comment on each other’s recognition.
Why valuesLinking each shout out to a company value turned values into visible actions new joiners could see. The most-praised colleagues earned a paid day off each quarter, and I tracked winners to keep it fair.

T2 Kidz: strategy & brand

T2 Kidz (Train Together London CIC) is a multi-sport club helping children across Sutton get active. I wrote its 2027 communications strategy, its tone of voice guide and a set of reusable social templates.

I built it around three audiences because the club depends on all three, and led with multi-sport discovery: its competitive advantage, and the answer to parents’ biggest worries about cost, logistics and committing to one sport too early.

Next: from 2027, I'll plan and create its social media strategy, alongside wider marketing and communications.

The challenge

Too few children in Sutton are active enough, and single-sport clubs add costs and travel that push families away. T2 Kidz needed one clear story for everyone it relies on.

Three audiences

  • →Parents & carers
  • →Young people looking for coaching experience
  • →Funders, schools & partners

What the strategy covers

  • →Goals and how we’ll measure them
  • →What each audience thinks now, and what we want them to think
  • →Content pillars, a termly calendar and channel plan
  • →A crisis protocol for session cancellations

Four content pillars

DISCOVEREDUCATEEMPOWERCONNECT

Every post, message and partner pack maps to one of four pillars, so the club speaks with one voice on every channel.

Tone of voice: “Be more human”

Four golden rules: read it out loud, make it two-way, zero corporate jargon and child and parent first, plus an emoji traffic light for each channel.

DON’T SAY“Please be advised that today’s session has been cancelled. Further information will be provided in due course.”
SAY THIS INSTEAD“Quick update: today’s session is cancelled. We’ll message you as soon as we have a new date. Any questions, reply here!”

Reusable social templates

Grid posts, Stories and Reels covers built in Canva. Children are blurred for privacy.
