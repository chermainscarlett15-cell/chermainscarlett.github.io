Sutton Scene, redesigned

Sutton Scene is the council's weekly resident newsletter, which I write and send. I audited seven months of performance — 571 stories across 32 issues — and presented my findings to management, recommending a mobile-first redesign for an audience that reads on their phones. The recommendation was approved, and I created the new design.

  • A cleaner, lighter masthead with the date up front
  • Quick links to Latest news and Local events
  • Single-column stories with clear, tappable buttons
  • A welcome from the Leader of the Council
